Principal Product Designer

I design for collaboration, shaping how humans and AI work together so neither has to yell for the other's attention. I design where it works, meeting people in the moment they're stuck, not asking them to leave and find help somewhere else. And I design in systems, so what I build holds up at scale and ships fast whether it's a team picking it up internally or millions of users touching it in production.

12+ years doing this for the experiences millions of people use daily. I got here sideways through filmmaking and computer science. Film taught me to construct a feeling you can't argue with. Code taught me to just build the thing.
