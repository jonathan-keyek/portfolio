TL;DR

Problem
The Coaching Compression Problem
A coach watches a skater and compresses everything they see into a vague cue: get lower, turn your foot. The real biomechanical signal is lost in translation. The skater hears words; the body needs data.
Solution
A Sensing Sock + Dashboard
A speculative smart sock captures foot pressure and angle in real time, and an iPad dashboard turns it into one specific, prioritized correction — not a dozen competing ones.
Outcome
Precision Coaching
The system replaces guesswork with a single actionable cue per rep, backed by data both coach and skater can see.

Key Features

The Sensing Sock

Pressure and angle sensors woven into the sock capture what the eye can't — weight distribution, edge angle, timing — without changing how the skater moves.

Smart sock concept: sensor placement and data capture

The Coach Dashboard

The iPad surfaces one prioritized correction at a time. Live biomechanical data, translated into a cue the coach can deliver in plain language.

iPad coaching dashboard: live data → single cue

Designing Within Physical Constraints

A sock can't have a screen, a battery brick, or a charging port that ruins the glide. Every interaction had to respect the physics of the thing on a foot.

Hardware constraint study: form factor and power

Deep Dive

Cognitive load on the ice

A skater mid-rep can act on one thing. The dashboard's job is to choose the highest-leverage correction and suppress the rest.
